You describe a chart twice: before a change and after it. VisDelta works out
what changed and draws every frame in between.

<LiveExample id="bar-remap" />

Drag the scrubber. Each position is a real chart: the bars, the axis and its
title always agree. Press play again at the end and the same frames run
backward.

## Three ideas

**A state is a value.** `bar(rows).x("product").y("revenue")` describes a chart.
It draws nothing and never changes. Calling `.y("profit")` on it gives you a
second state.

**A transition connects two states.** `transition(a, b)` matches the marks
that stay, the ones that leave and the ones that arrive, and plans a route
between them.

**Progress is just a number.** `progress(0.42)` shows one frame. A button, a
slider, scroll position or a timeline can supply that number. VisDelta never
decides when.

## What it is not

It does not clean data, guess which field should be colour, or morph one chart
type into another. A Bar state transitions to another Bar state.
